# D2PLUS Alpha V1.1

Alpha V1.1 brings more variety to unique drops, clearer item names, new top-tier artwork and the Accursed ritual. There is also a fresh gothic website, more polish around town, and a voice for Azmodan. The launcher, wiki and Android companion are updated together.

Read the [Accursed guide](https://d2plus-d2r.github.io/D2PLUS-Launcher/accursed.html) for the complete bonus pools and the two-transmute process. One of each seven Elder Gems makes a Ritual Sigil. An eligible unique plus a sigil has a 75% chance of one or two bonuses and a 25% chance of becoming a random rare of the same base.

Difficulty MF penalties: **−20 Normal / −50 Nightmare / −75 Hell**. New drops use the revised unique rolls; existing items keep their saved rolls. Successful Accursed items show a violet tooltip label; their inventory hue stays unchanged. Shared stash remains five tabs, with no D2RLoader.

Choose the Windows installer, portable launcher, or **Complete D2RMM** ZIP. The D2RMM package stands alone: disable older complete bundles and overlapping addons first. Keep your existing mod output name and back up offline saves before installing. Separate practice characters are optional and never replace your own saves.

The signed Android APK updates the v0.8-and-newer signed companion in place. Older v0.7 installations used a different key and need their app data backed up before uninstalling.

Alpha release: assets, recipes, stat limits and save round trips are checked; the new ritual, bush placement and Azmodan sound timing still need in-game playtesting. Keep Alpha V1.1 when loading items that carry its new Accursed stats.
