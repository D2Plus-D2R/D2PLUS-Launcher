Complete D2RMM mod, Windows launcher, Hero Editor and offline/online wiki. This release includes Shattered Sun, The Last Sunkeeper, Dungeon Reward Bags, revised high-end custom runewords and new item visuals.

D2PLUS Alpha V1.3

Charm weight and seven craftable Oaths are here. Your character can carry 50 charm weight before taking an overburden penalty. Check Advanced Character Stats for your total; each charm shows its own weight. Oaths share an aura with you and nearby allies, including your mercenary.

Unique Grand Charms can now be Accursed, crafting materials have orange names, and the launcher, Hero Editor, and both wikis include the new charm system.

[Read the announcement and update instructions](https://d2-plus.net/news-alpha-v13.html) · [Charm and Oath guide](https://d2-plus.net/wiki/charms.html) · [Complete D2RMM mod and Windows launcher](https://github.com/D2Plus-D2R/D2PLUS-Launcher/releases/tag/v1.3-alpha)

D2PLUS is an offline overhaul for Diablo II: Resurrected that gives you more to build, more to hunt, and more to do after the campaign.

It grew out of wanting to keep playing Diablo II without every character ending up on the same equipment path. The familiar campaign and item hunt are still here, but there are more skills worth trying, new uniques and sets to chase, additional bosses, and a crafting system that gives spare loot a purpose.

Sanctuary has also had quite a makeover. Dark stone, worn gold, weathered roses, new monsters, and a redesigned interface give D2PLUS its own look while keeping the atmosphere that makes Diablo feel like Diablo.

More ways to build your character

Want melee splash damage? Buy the optional melee splash charm from Gheed in Act I and keep it in your character’s inventory to enable the effect. Move it to your stash to turn it off.

All eight classes have skill changes, including the Warlock. Reworked attacks, adjusted scaling and synergies, and three additional five-rank passives per class open up different ways to spend your points.

The Amazon's Storm Lance launches lightning bolts that chain on impact. The Sorceress has Arcane Tempest, the Necromancer can recover life and mana through Soul Harvest, and the Barbarian gains options such as Storm Cry and Ancestral Standard. Paladin, Druid, Assassin, and Warlock builds have their own changes, with the wiki explaining how each skill works.

You can keep developing a character to level 100. The experience curve spreads that final climb across the later levels, with the heaviest investment near the cap. The companion tools include 33 build guides covering leveling, equipment, attributes, mercenaries, crafting, and useful Accursed outcomes.

Loot with more room for a great roll

D2PLUS adds custom uniques, class equipment, progression sets, endgame sets, and Diablo I-inspired relics alongside the existing item pool.

Alpha V1.1 gives custom uniques wider stat ranges, so finding another copy can still be exciting. Applicable class-skill bonuses that previously always gave +3 now roll from +1 to +3. Diablo I-inspired uniques have also received targeted improvements to help them stay useful.

Progression items have clearer names between tiers. The highest-tier custom uniques have their own new inventory artwork, and endgame set artwork has been resized where it was too small to read properly.

Seven level-95 class sets provide four-piece endgame targets. Completing a matching set activates its colored radiance effect around the character.

Existing equipment keeps its saved rolls when you update. The revised ranges apply to newly generated items.

A longer item hunt

D2PLUS is meant to give you strong equipment to work toward without handing out every upgrade immediately. Alpha V1.1 applies a Magic Find penalty of 20 in Normal, 50 in Nightmare, and 75 in Hell. These are penalties to your Magic Find stat, not flat percentage reductions to every drop.

There are also more places and reasons to farm. The wiki lists The Butcher and Azmodan for individual uniques and set pieces only where their difficulty, monster level, and loot table make those items possible.

The Accursed ritual

Once you have a useful unique, you can decide whether to keep it as it is or risk it for something better.

Cube one of each of the seven Elder Gems to create an Accursed Ritual Sigil. Place an eligible unique and the sigil in the Cube, then transmute twice. The first press prepares the attempt and stores its outcome. The second finishes the ritual.

Every attempt has the same odds:
- 40% chance to keep the unique and add one bonus.
- 35% chance to keep the unique and add two bonuses.
- 25% chance to lose the unique and receive a newly rolled rare of the same base item.

The bonus pool depends on the item. Weapons can gain bonuses such as attack speed, enhanced damage, Crushing Blow, or Deadly Strike. Wands, staves, and Sorceress orbs have a caster pool. Armor and jewelry have defensive and utility options, while jewels receive smaller bonuses.

A successful ritual preserves the unique's existing rolls and adds a violet Accursed label to its tooltip. It can succeed only once on that item. Sets, runewords, charms, and quest items are not eligible. Inventory artwork keeps its normal color.

It is a choice for spare gear or a character ready to gamble on an upgrade. A great result can finish a build, but your favorite item can become a rare.

Crafting that gives spare items a purpose

Artisan's Embers, Sovereign Seals, Elder Gems, custom runes, and the Horadric Cube connect much of the equipment progression.

Five matching Perfect Gems, an Artisan's Ember, and a Sovereign Seal create an Elder Gem. Those gems feed advanced crafting, companion upgrades, and the Accursed system.

Supported uniques and set items can be smelted with an Ember and a Seal to recover their assigned rune. Smelting destroys the item, so a duplicate can be useful even when you would never equip it.

Eight custom runes extend the rune ladder: Vyr, Nym, Pyr, Ael, Khar, Zyr, Thyr, and Eon. The reference tools cover all 163 enabled runewords, including existing and custom combinations, and include a planner for the runes you own.

Premium gambling provides another use for gold, with improved rare, set, and unique odds balanced by more expensive ring and amulet gambling.

New enemies in familiar places

The Butcher returns to Tristram alongside Griswold in Normal, Nightmare, and Hell. He has a custom model, cleaver, animations, voice lines, and combat sounds. He hits hard and has high defense, with lower resistances giving players other ways to deal with him.

His Normal loot uses Griswold's table. Nightmare uses Normal Andariel's regular table, and Hell uses Nightmare Andariel's regular table. A separate Uber Butcher appears only in Uber Tristram.

Azmodan the Lord of Sin appears beside Baal in all three difficulties, replacing the Act V Warden encounter. His four-legged model, attacks, animations, and sound effects give the Worldstone Chamber another threat. His loot follows Diablo's table for the matching difficulty, with a higher chance of dropping nothing.

More to do after Hell

The Furnace of Storms is a four-floor Hell dungeon with area levels 90, 92, 94, and 96. Its floors contain custom enemies, champions, and uniques, giving developed characters another place to test their builds and hunt equipment.

The Ashen Oath adds repeatable contracts for Andariel, Mephisto, Pindleskin, the Countess, and Baal. Collect 100 matching trophies for a contract to earn crafting materials and a reward token. Rewards depend on difficulty and include runes, with chances for additional gems or high runes.

The Secret Cow Level also has 32 unique trinkets to chase in Nightmare and Hell. Players and mercenaries can equip them in ring slots, creating more ways to customize a character or strengthen a companion.

Mercenaries worth investing in

Mercenaries have stronger baseline durability and their own Recruit, Veteran, and Oathbound equipment progression.

Custom appearances include a Blood Raven-inspired Act I companion, a cow warrior in Act II, a spellbook-carrying Act III sorcerer, and Doom Knight-style Act V companions.

Act III retains fire, cold, and lightning variants. These sorcerers use daggers and grimoires and have access to Cleave. Mercenaries also have two ring slots for ordinary rings or the custom trinkets.

A darker Sanctuary

Each town has its own treatment rather than sharing one texture replacement.

The Rogue Encampment has dark stone walls covered in rose vines, permanent stone shops for Akara, Charsi, and Gheed, a gothic meeting hall, a warrior statue, stone pillar lights, and decorative rose bushes.

Lut Gholein takes on a dark gothic Egyptian style. Kurast's stonework and buildings use darker materials and rose vines. The Pandemonium Fortress has dark gothic surfaces, roses, additional lighting, and a statue overlooking its central platform. Harrogath has darker buildings, much more snow, and heavier snowfall.

The Arcane Sanctuary has its own gothic overhaul, while the Cairn Stones use dark stone and gold detailing. The Warlock has a demonic appearance with redesigned garments and bright purple eyes.

A refreshed interface

The Gothic Rose HUD keeps the familiar health and mana orbs, with embellished angel and demon figures, dark stone, aged metal, and weathered roses.

Custom potion graphics make health, mana, and rejuvenation potions easier to distinguish. Rejuvenation potions have increased weighting in monster potion drops. Rune artwork, Sunder charm images, the Cube, and equipment sprites have also received attention.

The stash includes support for custom runes and crafting materials. Shared storage remains at five tabs. The complete package does not require D2RLoader.

Character selection, introductory artwork, and the loading splash have been updated to fit the D2PLUS theme, with the splash showing Alpha V1.3.

Tools that come with the mod

The Windows launcher bundles the gameplay files, offline wiki, hero editor, and an optional damage-number companion. It provides guided installation and keeps the previous mod output as a backup when updating.

The wiki includes item rolls, recipes, skill changes, farming routes, build guides, and a full Accursed reference. The hero editor includes the current item identities, artwork, and Accursed stat support.

The Android companion brings the reference material to your phone, including items, builds, rune planning, and the Accursed guide. It shares the website's dark stone theme and new D2PLUS emblem. It is a companion app, not an Android port of Diablo II.

Four separate practice characters are available with Cubes, Elder Gems, sigils, and spare uniques for trying the Accursed system.

Installation and current status

D2PLUS requires your own Diablo II: Resurrected installation with Reign of the Warlock and is intended for offline single-player.

Use the Windows launcher for guided installation, or install the Complete D2RMM package as one mod. The complete package includes the accumulated D2PLUS content and does not need an older release or the separate test addons. Disable overlapping D2PLUS packages before installing it.

Back up your characters and shared stash before updating, and keep your existing mod output name so the game continues using the same save location.

This is an active alpha. Package checks, item limits, and save-format tests have passed, but the newest Accursed gameplay, rose-bush placement, and Azmodan sound timing still need further in-game testing. Balance and visual polish will continue to change as people play.

D2PLUS is for anyone who still enjoys making one more run, finding one more upgrade, and seeing how far a favorite character can go.
