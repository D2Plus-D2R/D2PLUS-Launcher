D2PLUS Alpha V1.3 — Charm Weight and Oaths
October 26, 2026

CHOOSE YOUR CHARMS
- Charm capacity is 50. Every point above 50 applies a 15% penalty to maximum life and mana, capped at 90%.
- Magic Small / Large / Grand Charms weigh 1 / 2 / 3. Unique and Sunder charms weigh 5. The optional melee-splash charm weighs 1. Crafted Oaths weigh 7.
- Each charm tooltip shows that item's weight. Advanced Character Stats shows your total and limit.
- Existing charms missing a weight line can be updated in the Cube with one Scroll of Identify, without rerolling their existing bonuses.

SEVEN CRAFTABLE OATHS
- Combine one magic Grand Charm, three matching Elder Gems and one Worldforge Shard. Transmute twice to complete the Oath.
- Guardian's / Warlord's / Oracle's / Wayfarer's / Winter's / Stormcaller's / Gravekeeper's Oath: Diamond / Ruby / Amethyst / Emerald / Sapphire / Topaz / Skull respectively.
- Oaths require character level 75 and roll an aura level from 1 to 20. Each tooltip shows the exact bonuses for its rolled level.
- The aura benefits you and nearby allies, including your mercenary. Only one Oath can be carried at a time, across all seven types. Keep spare Oaths in shared storage.

ACCURSED GRAND CHARMS
- Unique Grand Charms, including Oaths, are now eligible for the one-attempt Accursed ritual.
- Success adds one charm weight: an Oath goes from 7 to 8. A brick returns a magic charm of the same base and removes the unique powers.
- Small and large unique charms remain ineligible. The ritual does not reroll the original Oath aura level.

TOOLS AND PRESENTATION
- Elder Gems and supported crafting materials have orange names.
- Launcher and game splashes now read Alpha V1.3.
- Hero Editor understands the new charm stats, seven Oaths and exact aura templates.
- Offline and online wikis include the weight rules, recipes and all 20 aura levels.
- Existing controller HUD improvements, save tools and offline-map rerolling remain included.

HOW TO UPDATE
1. Close D2R and back up your characters and shared stash.
2. D2RMM users: extract D2PLUS_Alpha_v13_Complete into D2RMM/mods, disable the older complete D2PLUS package and overlapping addons, enable D2PLUS - Alpha V1.3 Complete, then click Install Mods.
3. Launcher users: install the new launcher, open Setup, confirm D2R.exe and the existing mod output, then click Install Alpha V1.3.
4. Keep the same output mod name and save path. Existing characters can continue. Check your charm total before heading out.

This is a complete standalone D2RMM package; no earlier D2PLUS version, runtime plugin or extra loader is required. The Installed_Snapshot ZIP is for the launcher, not a D2RMM mod folder. No personal saves are included.
