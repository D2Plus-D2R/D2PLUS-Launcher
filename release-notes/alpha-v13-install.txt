D2PLUS Alpha V1.3 — D2RMM installation

1. Close Diablo II: Resurrected. Back up your characters and shared stash.
2. Extract this ZIP. Put the D2PLUS_Alpha_v13_Complete folder inside D2RMM's mods folder. That folder must directly contain mod.json and mod.js.
3. In D2RMM, disable your older complete D2PLUS package and overlapping addons. Enable D2PLUS - Alpha V1.3 Complete.
4. Click Install Mods, then launch with your usual D2RMM settings.

Keep the same output mod name and save path when updating. Do not delete or replace your save folder. No earlier D2PLUS installation or extra loader is needed.

Before playing, open Advanced Character Stats and check your charm weight. Capacity is 50. Move excess charms into storage to clear the overburden penalty.
For an older charm missing its weight line, put that charm and one Scroll of Identify in the Cube and transmute; existing rolls are preserved.

Oath recipe: one magic Grand Charm + three matching Elder Gems + one Worldforge Shard. Transmute twice. Only one Oath may be carried; keep spare Oaths in shared storage.
