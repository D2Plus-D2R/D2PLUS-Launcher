D2PLUS class skills: the Alpha V2.0a reference

Alpha V2.0a retains the skill roles introduced in V2.0. This guide explains those completed skills and the difference between invested points and total skill levels, starting with the Amazon’s Storm Lance.

Amazon: Storm Lance grows to seven bolts

Storm Lance adds two primary bolts at each milestone: total skill level 10, 20 and 30. Its primary volley starts with one bolt and reaches seven at level 30.

The wider fan makes the extra projectiles easier to see and spread across a group. Each primary bolt combines the improved flat lightning component with 50% weapon damage. A hit briefly stuns eligible targets for 0.24 seconds; this does not bypass a target’s stun immunity.

The impact chain is separate: it uses 25% of the skill’s lightning component. Do not count every chain hit as another full 50% weapon hit when comparing equipment. The bolt milestones use total skill level, including applicable skill bonuses.

Warlock: Sigils, Energy and demon support

Sigil Lord

Each effective rank grants 3% faster cast rate and 3% maximum mana, with each bonus capped at 15%. Five invested ranks allow a second active Sigil of each type. You still place the Sigils yourself; the passive does not automatically create them.

Astral Communion

The passive adds Enhanced Damage to Cleave and Echoing Strike from total Energy and invested ranks, capped at 200%. This adds to the existing damage calculation. Its separate mana-regeneration bonus caps at 100%; maximum mana and mana regeneration are different effects.

Demonic Resonance

The owner receives bounded crushing-blow, attack-speed and physical-damage-reduction support. Newly summoned or bound demons gain 5% Enhanced Damage per invested rank, capped at 25%. Tainted receive fire-skill damage; other demons receive physical Enhanced Damage.

Recast or rebind after changing invested ranks so the demon receives the current bonus. These additions do not turn every Warlock bonus into a shared aura.

Use the current ranks when planning a build

Invested ranks are the points you put into a skill. Total skill level also includes applicable bonuses from equipment and other sources. They can control different parts of the same skill: Storm Lance’s bolt milestones use total level, while Sigil Lord’s second-Sigil unlock needs five invested ranks.

The full reference covers eight classes, including Warlock. Browse the class skill tables or the wiki skill reference to compare the rest of the trees. The wiki’s build plans are data-checked theorycraft; they are not measured clear-time or damage benchmarks.
