AlphaV0.8.2 brings the current D2PLUS gameplay and companion tools together.

  • A new monster guards the act bosses, featuring a custom model.
  • Warden claws gain a fire effect and added fire damage equal to 20% of physical damage; all six Warden definitions have 20% more life in Normal, Nightmare and Hell. Andariel and the other act-boss models keep their own appearances.

  • 68 equipment appearances receive custom inventory art and/or visual treatment, including class capstone sets and Diablo I-inspired uniques. Long capstone set-name localization keys are repaired.
  • Updated skill-panel border/background and corrected numeric tooltips for Storm Lance, Arcane Tempest, Soul Harvest and Ancestral Standard.
  • Wiki and hero editor now share the current unique, set and skill names. All 33 build guides are revised for the current skills, with explicit prerequisites and legal point budgets.
  • Complete fixed-settings D2RMM package, bundled Windows installer and portable launcher, offline wiki and Android companion are synchronized to AlphaV0.8.2. Rune/material stash tabs, crafting, mercenaries and all 163 enabled runewords remain included.

Why D2PLUS changes skills

What is new in AlphaV0.8.2?

Storm Lance now launches aimed bolts from range. It gains extra primary bolts at skill levels 10 and 20, adds a 50% weapon contribution to those bolts, and produces impact chains using 25% of its skill lightning damage. The Amazon tab explains the separate damage components and weapon choices.

Clearer skill tooltips: Storm Lance now shows its bolt count; Arcane Tempest shows its strike interval; Soul Harvest shows recovery chance and life/mana per corpse; Ancestral Standard shows enemy slow and physical resistance reduction. These display fixes make the existing mechanics easier to plan around.

The detailed class changes below include the earlier D2PLUS reworks that remain active in this release. They are not all new changes in 0.8.2.

D2PLUS strengthens underused skills and puts more damage into the skill itself. Lower synergy requirements leave more points for a second attack, summons, or utility.

Each class gets three new passives with five spendable ranks each. Several existing skills become new attacks or support abilities.

Build tips

Build tips

  • Pick a main attack and one backup that solves a real problem. Maxing all three new passives still costs 15 points.
  • Attack, spell, and pet damage use different bonuses. Leech, Crushing Blow, and Deadly Strike do not turn ordinary spells into weapon hits.
  • Resistance reduction is damage-type specific and does not automatically break every immunity.
  • Corpse recovery and wards compete with Corpse Explosion and summons. Plan for fights before the first corpse.
  • Cast rate, attack speed, and hit recovery depend on breakpoints. Skill scaling can also hit a cap.

Storm Lance replaces Power Strike

What changed: the old melee strike is now an aimed ranged release. You can launch it toward the cursor without moving into melee or first selecting an enemy. It keeps the level-six unlock, costs 4 mana, and works with spears or javelins.

MechanicAlphaV0.8.2 behavior
Primary boltsOne at skill levels 1–9, two at 10–19, and three at 20 or higher. Equipment skill bonuses count toward these thresholds.
Primary damageFull skill lightning damage plus 50% weapon damage. Spears use melee weapon damage; javelins use throwing damage. The native weapon contribution also carries equipment elemental damage at 50% and does not convert physical damage into lightning.
Impact chainsUp to three follow-up jumps. Each uses 25% of the skill lightning component, without the added weapon contribution. This is not 25% of the preceding jump.
SynergyLightning Strike adds 5% skill lightning damage per hard rank. It remains a separate skill.

Why it changed: ranged aiming lets a spear or javelin Amazon engage packs without being forced into melee. Extra bolts reward skill investment, while the primary weapon component keeps weapon upgrades relevant. Chains retain a smaller, skill-only damage component instead of repeating the weapon bonus on every jump.

Build tradeoffs: keep Fend or another attack for situations that suit it. Javelins can also support Lightning Fury; native javelins have no sockets, so weapon runewords need a compatible spear base. Overlapping missiles share the existing hit-delay rules, so three bolts do not guarantee triple damage to one target. Leech and other on-hit interactions still need in-game confirmation.

Arcane Tempest replaces Thunder Storm

Frequent strikes make it useful alongside Nova, Lightning, or Enchant. The cost is refreshing it more often.

Judgment replaces Conversion

A delayed magic-damage column followed by holy bolts. Costs 12 mana with a 0.8-second delay. Primary damage starts at 80–120 and grows with levels; Holy Bolt grants 5% primary damage per hard rank.

Use it with Holy Bolt or Fist of the Heavens, or as a magic backup on a weapon build. Weapon damage and Conviction do not boost the primary magic spell. Secondary holy bolts retain target restrictions.

Questions at the gate

Does the launcher install the gameplay mod?

Yes. The AlphaV0.8.2 launcher bundles the complete gameplay snapshot. Select your D2R.exe, then click Install AlphaV0.8.2. A separate all-in-one D2RMM mod remains available.

What does resetting maps do?

The launcher passes D2R’s offline-map reset argument. While enabled, new games reroll maps. It does not delete character files. Changing the option requires relaunching the game.

What has been tested?

Automated data, launcher and package checks accompany this release. Live D2R gameplay, Windows display scaling and Android device behavior still need player testing.

Where can I report an issue?

Use the GitHub repository’s bug-report form. Include your version, selected options and steps to reproduce. Remove personal information from logs.
