Alpha V2.0 · November 7, 2026

New depths below Sanctuary.

A new descent, a stronger monster at the end of the tunnel, and more ways to build the hero who goes after it. Alpha V2.0 brings our recent dungeons, boss improvements and skill work together in one complete release.

Descend into new dungeons

  • Gloamweb opens a separate spider-cavern route with a final boss chamber and a stronger Uber counterpart. Vharzith, the Hollow Weaver uses poison and disruptive attacks to punish careless approaches.
  • The Nightmare Sanctum is ruled by Azrakek, First Nightmare; its endgame counterpart faces you with Uber Azrakek. Kharvul, the Cindermaw rules The Cinderwound, an optional early dungeon in The Hole with stronger Nightmare and Hell encounters.
  • These optional routes preserve campaign quest destinations. Cube-entry routes are separate from campaign progression; follow their recipe tooltips.
  • Eligible bosses have a flat 10% base Dungeon Reward Bag roll. Normal, Nightmare and Hell select their matching bag tier. High-end contents remain rare.

Open the gates in Harrogath

Each Cube recipe uses two matching gems, one Sovereign Seal and one Artisan’s Ember. The lower-tier portals unlock on Nightmare and also work on Hell; Uber entries require Hell.

Bosses with more bite

Custom bosses receive rougher materials, more natural surface detail and attack-animation refinements. The new cave boss also receives a difficulty pass on all three settings. These are continuing alpha improvements; share video and the selected difficulty when reporting an attack or visual problem.

Finished skill roles

  • Storm Lance grows to seven primary bolts at total skill level 30, with a wider spread, stronger flat lightning, 50% weapon contribution and brief stun on eligible targets.
  • Shadow Rift becomes a short magic shock ring around the Assassin, while Judgment becomes a targeted magic spell with consistent secondary bolts.
  • Arcane Tempest strikes with a small lightning splash. Soul Harvest becomes an active recovery stance, and Ancestral Standard supports nearby allies.
  • Bloodroot consumes corpses while its owner is wounded, healing the Druid and erupting for physical damage. Ravage becomes a heavy wolf-or-bear strike with knockback and bounded life and mana recovery on successful hits; normal target-drain rules still apply.
  • Sigil Lord grants 3% faster cast rate and maximum mana per effective rank, capped at 15% each. Five invested ranks permit a second active Sigil of each type; you still place them manually.
  • Astral Communion adds Enhanced Damage to Cleave and Echoing Strike from total Energy and invested ranks, capped at 200%. Its separate mana regeneration caps at 100%. These bonuses add to the existing damage calculation.
  • Demonic Resonance supports the owner and newly summoned or bound demons. Each invested rank adds 5% enhanced demon damage, up to 25%; Tainted receive fire-skill damage and other demons physical damage. Recast or rebind after changing its invested ranks.
  • Current descriptions and point limits are shared by the website, Hero Editor and wiki. Hard ranks and total skill levels can affect different bonuses; use the current tooltip when planning.

A familiar interface, refined

  • The enemy health bar is twice as wide, centered near the bottom, and follows valid-target visibility.
  • Ordinary gems have a distinct cyan name color. Elder Gems keep their orange names.
  • A new D2PLUS castle loading screen retains the opening-door animation and light from beyond the gates.
  • The Windows launcher, Hero Editor, online/offline wiki and Android reference move to Alpha V2.0 together.

From the latest journeys

One complete download

  1. Close D2R and back up your offline character and shared-stash files.
  2. Launcher users: install the updated launcher, select your D2R.exe, then use Install Alpha V2.0. Installing the launcher alone does not replace gameplay until that step runs.
  3. D2RMM users: extract the complete package into D2RMM/mods, disable older complete packages and overlapping test add-ons, enable the new complete D2PLUS mod and click Install Mods. No earlier D2PLUS download is needed.
  4. Keep the same installed output name and save path to continue existing characters, then start a fresh game session.

The mod requires your own compatible Diablo II: Resurrected installation. Character saves and game executables are not distributed.
