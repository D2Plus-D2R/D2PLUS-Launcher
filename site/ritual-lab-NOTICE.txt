D2PLUS Accursed practice chamber

This browser simulation uses the D2PLUS public wiki's unique item names, item
artwork paths, and source item properties. The checked-in example catalog was
generated from https://d2-plus.net/wiki/data.js; its source SHA-256 and item-table
provenance are recorded inside ritual-lab-catalog.json.

The recipe rows in ritual-lab-rules.json and the eligible base-type mapping in
ritual-lab-pools.json mirror the shipped D2PLUS Alpha V1.2 Accursed rules. Recipe
odds and item ranges retain the current wiki's Alpha V1.1 data provenance.

No game save parser, save writer, character upload, or exchange mutation is
included. Original examples use fixed legal rolls; the ritual outcome and bonus
rolls are random. Successful examples preserve their original properties. The
rare failure outcome discards unique powers and deliberately does not simulate
the separate game's rare-affix generation. Base weapon damage and armor defense
are not recalculated; displayed values are item modifiers.

Regenerate examples:
  python build/ritual_lab_data.py --wiki docs/wiki/database.json

Verify the model and browser interactions (the repository's jsdom devDependency):
  node --test test/ritual-lab.test.mjs test/ritual-lab-ui.test.mjs

The public build refreshes the compact catalog from the generated wiki database
and includes the same chamber in accursed.html and wiki/accursed.html.
