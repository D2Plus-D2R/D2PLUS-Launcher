ALPHA V1.3 · OCTOBER 26, 2026

A little weight.
A lot of choice.

Alpha V1.3 gives you a new reason to think about the charms you carry. We have added charm weight, seven craftable Oaths that help you and your allies, and clearer tooltips so you can see what each charm is doing.

Charm weight, in plain terms

Think of it as a budget for charm bonuses. Your character has a weight limit of 50. Each charm uses some of that budget while it is in your inventory. You can still put charms anywhere in your inventory; there is no special charm-only area.

At 50 or below, there is no overburden penalty. Each point above 50 applies a 15% penalty to maximum life and mana, up to a 90% penalty. Weight 51 means a 15% penalty; weight 52 means 30%; weight 56 reaches the cap. If your globes suddenly look much smaller after adding a charm, check your weight.

Hover a charm to see its own weight. Open Advanced Character Stats to see your total and limit. Move charms into storage to bring the total back down.

Craft an Oath for your party

Put one magic Grand Charm, three matching Elder Gems, and one Worldforge Shard in the Horadric Cube. Press Transmute twice to finish your Oath. The gem type determines which of the seven Oaths you receive.

Oaths require level 75 and roll an aura level from 1 to 20. A higher roll gives stronger bonuses, and the tooltip shows the exact benefits for that roll. The aura helps you and nearby allies, including your mercenary.

You can carry only one Oath at a time, regardless of type. Keep spare Oaths in shared storage. Each weighs 7, so leave room in your charm budget before adding one. Recipes and every aura level are listed in the Charm & Oath guide.

A new risk for unique Grand Charms

Unique Grand Charms can now take part in the Accursed ritual. A successful attempt adds one weight along with its bonus: an Oath goes from 7 to 8. If it bricks, it becomes a magic charm of the same base and loses its unique powers. The original aura level is not rerolled on a successful ritual. Small and large unique charms are still excluded.

A few other changes

Elder Gems and supported crafting materials now have orange names to make them easier to recognise. The launcher and game splashes read Alpha V1.3, and the Hero Editor and both wikis have been updated for the charm system.

Updating your game

  1. Close D2R and back up your characters and shared stash.
  2. D2RMM: extract the new complete package into D2RMM/mods, disable the older complete D2PLUS package and overlapping addons, enable Alpha V1.3 Complete, and click Install Mods.
  3. Launcher: install the new launcher, check your existing game and mod paths in Setup, then click Install Alpha V1.3.

Keep your existing output mod name and save path. You can continue with your current characters, and the charm system needs no runtime plugin or extra loader.

If an older charm has no weight line, cube it with one Scroll of Identify to update it without rerolling its existing bonuses. Check your total weight before your next run.
