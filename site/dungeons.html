D2PLUS dungeons, bosses and portal recipes

A spider cavern, a nightmare beyond the castle gates, and a hellish descent beneath Act I. D2PLUS adds optional encounters to your offline adventure, with stronger versions for heroes who return on higher difficulties.

Open the Cube portals in Harrogath

Every recipe below uses two matching gems, one Sovereign Seal and one Artisan’s Ember. Transmute them in the Horadric Cube in Harrogath. The gem tier chooses the destination; the game difficulty must also meet the entry rule.

The lower-tier portals also open in a Hell game. The Uber entries require Hell and their Elder Gem recipe. These Cube routes are separate from campaign quest destinations.

Gloamweb Hollows and the Gloamweb Abyss

Gloamweb is a single sprawling spider cavern that leads into a final chamber. More spiders and champion packs make the approach part of the encounter. Vharzith, the Hollow Weaver waits at the end, using poison and disruptive attacks to punish an unprepared approach.

The Abyss keeps the same dungeon setup and raises the strength of the monsters and its boss. Use the Perfect Emerald route to meet the Hollow Weaver first; return with Elder Emeralds for Uber Vharzith and the stronger loot tier.

Nightmare Sanctum and Uber Azrakek

Azrakek, First Nightmare rules the Perfect Amethyst entry. The original endgame Sanctum uses Elder Amethysts and faces you with Uber Azrakek. The two recipes let you choose the encounter tier without changing campaign progress.

Bring the full recipe to Harrogath and check both the gems and the selected game difficulty if the Cube will not open a portal. Two Perfect Amethysts belong to the Nightmare Sanctum recipe; two Elder Amethysts belong to the Hell-only endgame entry.

The Cinderwound: an early Act I dungeon

The optional Hole cave becomes The Cinderwound. It keeps its existing world entrance and cave connections, so you find it while exploring Act I rather than through a Cube portal. Campaign quest destinations stay intact.

Kharvul, the Cindermaw is its boss. The Normal version provides an early dungeon encounter, with much harder Nightmare and Hell versions. The V2.0 release added a difficulty pass across all three settings, plus attack-animation and material work.

Dungeon Reward Bags: the current drop rule

Eligible bosses have a flat 10% base chance to drop a Dungeon Reward Bag. The bag tier follows the encounter’s existing reward table. The Cinderwound uses Normal, Nightmare and Hell bags on their matching game difficulties. The lower-tier Gloamweb Hollows and Nightmare Sanctum retain their Nightmare bags even when opened in a Hell game; their Uber counterparts use Hell bags. This is one independent roll, rather than a promise that a bag will appear within ten kills.

Alpha V2.0a corrects a table-loading error that could make the bag drop every time. The 10% roll is independent of the offline Players setting. It replaces the older player-scaled drop-rate figures in historical notes. High-end bag contents remain rare. Read the bag’s tooltip for its opening recipe and prepared state, and use the current reward-bag guide for the full Cube instructions.
